TITLE: Identification of Nitrogen (N) in Biomolecules
SUBJECT: biology
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Biomolecules
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Perform the Biuret test on protein-rich egg white and fish extract to confirm the presence of nitrogen in biomolecules.

MATERIALS:
- Egg white
- Fish extract solution
- Sodium hydroxide (NaOH) solution
- Copper sulphate (CuSO4) solution
- Test tubes
- Dropper

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Understand that nitrogen is a key component of amino acids and proteins
- Perform the Biuret test (NaOH + CuSO4) to detect peptide bonds

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Pour 2 mL of egg white solution into a test tube.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: egg_white
DROP_TARGET_ID: test_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #fef3c7
RESULT_STATE: egg_white_solution
WHY: Egg white is composed primarily of ovalbumin, a nitrogen-rich protein.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Add 2 mL of sodium hydroxide (NaOH) solution to the test tube.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: naoh_solution
DROP_TARGET_ID: test_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #f8fafc
RESULT_STATE: alkaline_egg_white_mixture
WHY: Sodium hydroxide creates an alkaline environment necessary for the Biuret complex to form.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Add 5 drops of copper sulphate (CuSO4) solution and mix gently.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: cuso4_solution
DROP_TARGET_ID: test_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #7e22ce
RESULT_STATE: violet_biuret_complex
WHY: Cu2+ ions coordinate with peptide bond nitrogen atoms in alkaline conditions.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: What color does the mixture turn upon adding copper sulphate?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Purple / Violet|Bright Green|Milky White|Deep Red
CORRECT_OPTION: Purple / Violet
RESULT_COLOR: #7e22ce
RESULT_STATE: violet_biuret_complex
HINT: A violet or purple solution confirms protein and peptide nitrogen.
WHY: Cu2+ forms a purple coordination complex with nitrogen in peptide linkages (-CO-NH-), confirming nitrogen in proteins.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: The formation of a purple (violet) color in the Biuret test confirms the presence of protein and peptide-bound nitrogen in egg white and fish.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why does carbohydrates (like starch) fail to produce a purple color with Biuret reagent?
- Which fundamental building block of proteins contains nitrogen?
