TITLE: Identifying Monocot and Dicot Plants
SUBJECT: biology
GRADE: 10
UNIT: The World of Life (Unit 13)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Compare root systems, leaf venation, and stem branching patterns between monocotyledonous (e.g. Grass/Paddy) and dicotyledonous (e.g. Kuppameniya) angiosperms.

MATERIALS:
- Uprooted Grass / Paddy plant (Monocot)
- Uprooted Kuppameniya (Acalypha) plant (Dicot)
- Water basin / wash trough
- Hand lens / magnifying glass
- White observation sheet

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Contrast fibrous root systems with tap root systems
- Differentiate parallel leaf venation from reticulate (net) venation
- Classify flowering plants (Angiosperms) into Monocots and Dicots based on morphological traits

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Wash the root systems of both plants carefully in a water basin to remove clinging soil particles.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: grass_plant
DROP_TARGET_ID: water_basin
RESULT_COLOR: #f0fdf4
RESULT_STATE: clean_root_specimens
WHY: Washing reveals the branching root architecture clearly without damaging delicate rootlets.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Place the Grass / Paddy specimen on the white sheet and examine its root architecture and leaf venation.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: grass_plant
DROP_TARGET_ID: observation_sheet
RESULT_COLOR: #dcfce7
RESULT_STATE: monocot_fibrous_roots_parallel_veins
WHY: Monocots possess adventitious fibrous root systems originating from the base of the stem and parallel leaf venation.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Place the Kuppameniya specimen on the observation sheet and inspect its primary root and leaves.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: kuppameniya_plant
DROP_TARGET_ID: observation_sheet
RESULT_COLOR: #bbf7d0
RESULT_STATE: dicot_tap_root_reticulate_veins
WHY: Dicots exhibit a prominent primary taproot with lateral roots and a branched reticulate network of veins on broad leaves.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Which combination of morphological features accurately identifies a Monocotyledonous plant like Grass?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Fibrous roots and parallel leaf venation|Tap root system and reticulate venation|Branched woody stem and net veins|Two seed leaves and branched tap root
CORRECT_OPTION: Fibrous roots and parallel leaf venation
RESULT_COLOR: #16a34a
RESULT_STATE: monocot_traits_confirmed
HINT: Look at the root cluster arising from one point and the straight, parallel veins extending along the blade.
WHY: Monocots are characterized by a single cotyledon, fibrous root system, unbranched hollow/jointed stems, and parallel venation.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Monocots (such as Paddy and Grass) feature fibrous root systems and parallel leaf venation, whereas Dicots (such as Kuppameniya) feature taproot systems and reticulate (net-like) venation.
