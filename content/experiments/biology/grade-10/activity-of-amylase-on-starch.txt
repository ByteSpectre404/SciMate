TITLE: Activity of Amylase on Starch
SUBJECT: biology
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Biomolecules & Enzymes
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Observe the enzymatic digestion of starch into simpler sugars using amylase from germinating green gram seedlings and test with iodine.

MATERIALS:
- Starch (flour) solution
- Amylase solution (green gram extract)
- Test tube
- White porcelain tile
- Iodine solution
- Stopwatch

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Demonstrate how the enzyme amylase breaks down starch over time
- Understand the function of iodine as a specific indicator for starch
- Explain the role of enzymes as biological catalysts

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Pour 2 mL of starch solution into a clean test tube.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: starch_solution
DROP_TARGET_ID: test_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #fef08a
RESULT_STATE: translucent_starch_solution
WHY: Starch serves as the substrate for the enzyme amylase.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Add 2 mL of amylase solution into the test tube with starch and mix well.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: amylase_solution
DROP_TARGET_ID: test_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #fef3c7
RESULT_STATE: starch_amylase_mixture
WHY: Amylase begins breaking down the complex starch molecules into simpler maltose sugars upon mixing.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Place one drop of the reaction mixture onto the white porcelain tile.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: test_tube
DROP_TARGET_ID: porcelain_tile
RESULT_COLOR: #fef3c7
RESULT_STATE: sample_drop_on_tile
WHY: Sampling onto a tile allows clear observation of the color reaction with iodine indicator.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Add one drop of iodine solution to the sample drop on the tile at 0 minutes.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: iodine_solution
DROP_TARGET_ID: porcelain_tile
RESULT_COLOR: #1e1b4b
RESULT_STATE: blue_black_complex
WHY: At time zero, unreacted starch is present, turning the iodine dark blue-black.

===STEP===
ID: step-5
INSTRUCTION: What happens to the iodine color after 20 minutes of amylase action?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Turns dark blue-black|Stays pale yellow/no color change|Turns bright pink|Turns milky white
CORRECT_OPTION: Stays pale yellow/no color change
RESULT_COLOR: #b45309
RESULT_STATE: iodine_yellow_brown
HINT: As time passes, amylase breaks down all the starch so no blue-black reaction occurs.
WHY: All the starch has been digested into simple sugars by amylase, so iodine no longer changes to blue-black.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Amylase digests starch into simpler sugars. As starch is broken down over time, it no longer reacts with iodine, confirming enzyme activity.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why does iodine turn blue-black initially but stay yellow/brown after 20 minutes?
- How would boiling the amylase solution beforehand affect this experiment?
