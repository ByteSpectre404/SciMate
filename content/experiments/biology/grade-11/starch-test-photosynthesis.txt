TITLE: Identification of Starch in Leaves (Starch Test)
SUBJECT: biology
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Photosynthesis
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Test a leaf that was exposed to sunlight to confirm the production of starch during photosynthesis using iodine solution.

MATERIALS:
- Beaker with water
- Test tube with alcohol
- Tripod and Bunsen burner
- Plucked green leaf
- Iodine solution
- White tile
- Forceps

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Demonstrate how to decolorize a leaf safely using a boiling alcohol water bath
- Test for starch using iodine solution as a proof of photosynthesis

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Boil the plucked green leaf in a beaker of water for 2 minutes.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: green_leaf
DROP_TARGET_ID: boiling_water_beaker
RESULT_COLOR: #16a34a
RESULT_STATE: softened_soft_tissue
WHY: Boiling breaks down cell membranes and stops enzymatic reactions within leaf cells.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Place the boiled leaf into a test tube containing alcohol and heat in a water bath.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: green_leaf
DROP_TARGET_ID: alcohol_test_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #fef3c7
RESULT_STATE: decolorized_pale_leaf
WHY: Alcohol dissolves and removes green chlorophyll pigment from the leaf cells so color changes can be observed.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Wash the decolorized pale leaf in warm water and place on a white tile.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: decolorized_leaf
DROP_TARGET_ID: white_tile
RESULT_COLOR: #fef08a
RESULT_STATE: softened_flat_leaf
WHY: Washing softens the leaf hardened by alcohol and flattens it for indicator testing.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Add a few drops of iodine solution to the leaf on the white tile.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: iodine_solution
DROP_TARGET_ID: white_tile
RESULT_COLOR: #1e1b4b
RESULT_STATE: dark_blue_black_starch_complex
WHY: Iodine reacts specifically with starch produced by glucose condensation during photosynthesis.

===STEP===
ID: step-5
INSTRUCTION: What color does the leaf turn when iodine solution is added?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Dark Blue / Purple|Bright Pink|Remains Pale Yellow|Turns Milky White
CORRECT_OPTION: Dark Blue / Purple
RESULT_COLOR: #1e1b4b
RESULT_STATE: dark_blue_black_starch_complex
HINT: Iodine turns blue-black in the presence of starch.
WHY: Starch formed in the leaf during photosynthesis reacts with iodine to produce a characteristic dark blue-black/purple color.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: The leaf turns blue-black with iodine after chlorophyll removal, proving that glucose produced during photosynthesis was stored as starch.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why must alcohol be heated in a water bath rather than over a direct flame?
- What would happen to the iodine test if the plant was kept in the dark for 48 hours prior to the experiment?
