TITLE: Oxygen Production in Photosynthesis
SUBJECT: biology
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Photosynthesis
DIFFICULTY: hard
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Collect gas released by aquatic Hydrilla plants under sunlight and test it with a glowing splinter to identify oxygen gas.

MATERIALS:
- Water trough
- Glass funnel
- Boiling test tube
- Aquatic plant (Hydrilla)
- Water
- Wooden glowing splinter

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Collect oxygen gas produced by aquatic plants by water displacement
- Use a glowing splinter test to confirm oxygen gas

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Place Hydrilla plants inside an inverted glass funnel in a water trough.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: hydrilla_plant
DROP_TARGET_ID: glass_funnel
RESULT_COLOR: #16a34a
RESULT_STATE: submerged_plant
WHY: Hydrilla is an aquatic submerged plant that releases oxygen bubbles directly into water when photosynthesizing.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Invert a water-filled boiling test tube over the funnel stem.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: boiling_tube
DROP_TARGET_ID: glass_funnel_stem
RESULT_COLOR: #38bdf8
RESULT_STATE: inverted_water_filled_tube
WHY: Inverting a tube filled with water allows gas bubbles to rise and displace water at the top.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Place the set-up in bright sunlight and observe gas collecting at the top of the tube.
ACTION_TYPE: toggle_switch
DRAG_ITEM_ID: sunlight_source
DROP_TARGET_ID: setup
RESULT_COLOR: #e0f2fe
RESULT_STATE: effervescent
WHY: Photolysis of water in illuminated chlorophyll releases oxygen gas bubbles that rise up the funnel.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Insert a glowing wooden splinter into the collected gas at the top of the test tube.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: glowing_splinter
DROP_TARGET_ID: boiling_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #f59e0b
RESULT_STATE: reignited_flame
WHY: Oxygen supports combustion, causing a glowing ember to re-ignite into a bright flame.

===STEP===
ID: step-5
INSTRUCTION: What happens to the glowing splinter when inserted into the gas?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Re-ignites into a bright flame|Extinguishes with a pop sound|Turns green without flame|No reaction
CORRECT_OPTION: Re-ignites into a bright flame
RESULT_COLOR: #f59e0b
RESULT_STATE: reignited_flame
HINT: Oxygen rekindles a glowing ember.
WHY: Oxygen gas supports rapid combustion, reigniting the glowing splinter and proving O2 is produced during photosynthesis.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Gas bubbles collected from illuminated Hydrilla reignited a glowing splinter, confirming oxygen is evolved as a byproduct of photosynthesis.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why is Hydrilla suitable for demonstrating oxygen release compared to terrestrial plants?
- What chemical reaction in photosynthesis splits water molecules to release oxygen?
