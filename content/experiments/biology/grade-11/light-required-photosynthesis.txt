TITLE: Light Requirement for Photosynthesis
SUBJECT: biology
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Photosynthesis
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Prove that light energy is essential for photosynthesis using light-shielded (black polythene) and transparent-shielded plant leaves.

MATERIALS:
- Destarched potted plant
- Black polythene strip
- Colourless transparent polythene strip
- Paper clips
- Iodine solution & starch test apparatus

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Explain why plants must be destarched before photosynthesis experiments
- Demonstrate that light is an indispensable energy source for starch synthesis

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Cover leaf A with black polythene and leaf B with transparent polythene on the destarched plant.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: black_polythene
DROP_TARGET_ID: leaf_a
RESULT_COLOR: #1e293b
RESULT_STATE: light_shielded_leaf
WHY: Black polythene blocks sunlight while transparent polythene permits sunlight penetration as a control.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Expose the potted plant to bright sunlight for 4 hours.
ACTION_TYPE: toggle_switch
DRAG_ITEM_ID: sunlight_exposure
DROP_TARGET_ID: plant
RESULT_COLOR: #16a34a
RESULT_STATE: photosynthesizing_plant
WHY: Sunlight provides photons needed to excite electrons in chlorophyll and drive glucose synthesis.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Perform the iodine starch test on leaf A (covered with black polythene).
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: No color change (remains pale brown)|Turns dark blue-black|Turns bright blue|Turns green
CORRECT_OPTION: No color change (remains pale brown)
RESULT_COLOR: #fef08a
RESULT_STATE: remains_pale_brown
HINT: Without light, leaf A could not perform photosynthesis or store starch.
WHY: Light was blocked by black polythene, so no photosynthesis occurred and no starch was present to react with iodine.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Perform the iodine starch test on leaf B (covered with transparent polythene).
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Turns dark blue-black|No color change (remains pale brown)|Turns red|Turns white
CORRECT_OPTION: Turns dark blue-black
RESULT_COLOR: #1e1b4b
RESULT_STATE: dark_blue_black_starch_complex
HINT: Transparent polythene allowed light through for photosynthesis.
WHY: Light passed through transparent polythene enabling photosynthesis and starch formation, turning iodine dark blue-black.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Leaf area shielded from light showed no starch while light-exposed leaf area produced starch, proving light is required for photosynthesis.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why was the plant kept in darkness for 48 hours before starting the experiment?
- What role does light play in the light-dependent stage of photosynthesis?
