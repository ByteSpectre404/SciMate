TITLE: Bell Jar Model of Human Breathing Mechanism
SUBJECT: biology
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Biological Processes
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Demonstrate inspiration and expiration mechanisms using a bell jar, Y-tube, balloons (lungs), and rubber membrane (diaphragm).

MATERIALS:
- Glass bell jar
- Y-shaped glass tube
- Two rubber balloons
- Rubber sheet membrane
- Rubber bands

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Model thoracic cavity volume changes during ventilation
- Relate diaphragm movement to lung expansion (inspiration) and deflation (expiration)

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Pull the bottom rubber membrane (diaphragm) downwards away from the bell jar.
ACTION_TYPE: toggle_switch
DRAG_ITEM_ID: rubber_diaphragm_pull
DROP_TARGET_ID: bell_jar_model
RESULT_COLOR: #ef4444
RESULT_STATE: balloons_inflated
WHY: Pulling membrane down simulates diaphragm contraction flattening downward during inhalation.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Observe the balloons inside the bell jar when the membrane is pulled down. What happens?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Balloons inflate (fill with air)|Balloons deflate and collapse|Balloons burst|No change occurs
CORRECT_OPTION: Balloons inflate (fill with air)
RESULT_COLOR: #ef4444
RESULT_STATE: balloons_inflated
HINT: Expanding jar volume decreases internal pressure below atmospheric pressure.
WHY: Increasing bell jar volume lowers internal pressure, sucking atmospheric air through Y-tube into balloons (inspiration).

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Push the bottom rubber membrane upwards back into the bell jar base.
ACTION_TYPE: toggle_switch
DRAG_ITEM_ID: rubber_diaphragm_push
DROP_TARGET_ID: bell_jar_model
RESULT_COLOR: #fca5a5
RESULT_STATE: balloons_deflated
WHY: Pushing membrane up simulates diaphragm relaxing upward into dome shape during exhalation.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Observe balloons when membrane is pushed upwards. What happens?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Balloons deflate (expel air)|Balloons inflate larger|Balloons fill with liquid|No change
CORRECT_OPTION: Balloons deflate (expel air)
RESULT_COLOR: #fca5a5
RESULT_STATE: balloons_deflated
HINT: Decreasing jar volume increases internal pressure above atmospheric pressure.
WHY: Decreasing bell jar volume increases internal pressure, forcing air out of balloons through Y-tube (expiration).

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Pulling the diaphragm down increases thoracic volume and inflates lungs (inhalation); releasing it decreases volume and deflates lungs (exhalation).

- What component of the human respiratory system does the glass bell jar represent?
- What are two limitations of this bell jar model compared to real human breathing?
