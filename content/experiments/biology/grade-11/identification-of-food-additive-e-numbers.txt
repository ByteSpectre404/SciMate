TITLE: Identification of Food Additives and E-Numbers
SUBJECT: biology
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Biosphere (Unit 15)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Audit packaged food ingredient labels to identify E-number food additives, classifying them into colourings, preservatives, antioxidants, emulsifiers, and flavour enhancers.

MATERIALS:
- Packaged food ingredient labels (soft drink cans, fruit cordials, instant noodles, biscuits, confectionery)
- International E-number Codex classification index:
  * 100 series: Food Colours (e.g. E102 Tartrazine, E110 Sunset Yellow)
  * 200 series: Preservatives (e.g. E211 Sodium benzoate, E220 Sulfur dioxide)
  * 300 series: Antioxidants & Acidity regulators (e.g. E300 Ascorbic acid, E330 Citric acid)
  * 400 series: Emulsifiers, Stabilizers & Thickeners (e.g. E412 Guar gum)
  * 600 series: Flavour Enhancers (e.g. E621 Monosodium glutamate / MSG)
- Food audit worksheet & magnifying glass

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Decipher international E-number codes on commercial food packaging
- Understand the technical functions of food additives (preservation, color stability, shelf-life extension)
- Recognize potential adverse health effects (hyperactivity, allergies, asthma triggers) associated with synthetic additives
