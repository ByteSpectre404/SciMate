TITLE: Microscopic Observation of Plant Tissues
SUBJECT: biology
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Living Tissues
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Prepare temporary slides of betel leaf epidermis, potato tuber, and balsam stem cross-section to observe meristematic and permanent plant tissues.

MATERIALS:
- Betel leaf lower epidermis peel
- Thin potato tuber section
- Balsam stem cross-section
- Light microscope
- Glass slides & coverslips
- Water dropper

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Prepare temporary wet mount slides for microscopic inspection
- Identify simple permanent tissues (parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma) and complex tissues (xylem, phloem)

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Place a thin peel of betel leaf epidermis onto a glass slide with a drop of water and coverslip.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: betel_epidermis_peel
DROP_TARGET_ID: glass_slide
RESULT_COLOR: #bbf7d0
RESULT_STATE: wet_mount_slide
WHY: Mounting in water prevents tissue drying and eliminates trapped air bubbles under the coverslip.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Mount the glass slide onto the microscope stage and focus under low power objective lens.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: prepared_slide
DROP_TARGET_ID: microscope_stage
RESULT_COLOR: #0284c7
RESULT_STATE: focused_microscope_view
WHY: Focusing under low power locates specimen field before switching to higher magnification.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Observe the thin potato tuber section under microscope. What stored substance is seen in parenchyma cells?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Starch grains inside parenchyma cells|Chloroplasts moving|Xylem vessels with lignin rings|Air cavities
CORRECT_OPTION: Starch grains inside parenchyma cells
RESULT_COLOR: #fef08a
RESULT_STATE: parenchyma_starch_amyloplasts
HINT: Potato tubers store carbohydrates in specialized storage parenchyma.
WHY: Potato tuber cells consist of unspecialized parenchyma filled with oval starch amyloplast grains.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Observe balsam stem cross section. Where are xylem vascular vessels located?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: In vascular bundles providing water transport and support|Only in outer cuticle layer|Spread randomly in air spaces|Inside stomata guard cells
CORRECT_OPTION: In vascular bundles providing water transport and support
RESULT_COLOR: #38bdf8
RESULT_STATE: vascular_bundle_xylem
HINT: Xylem and phloem form vascular bundles arranged in rings in dicot stems.
WHY: Xylem complex permanent tissue forms thick-walled conductive tubes within vascular bundles transporting water from roots.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Microscope examination reveals diverse plant tissues organized into simple permanent (parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma) and complex conducting tissues (xylem, phloem).
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- How do meristematic tissues differ fundamentally from permanent tissues in plants?
- What structural feature gives sclerenchyma cells high mechanical strength?
