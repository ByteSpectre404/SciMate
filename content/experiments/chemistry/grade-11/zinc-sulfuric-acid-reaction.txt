TITLE: Reaction Between Zinc and Dilute Sulfuric Acid
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Electrochemistry (Unit 12)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Observe direct redox electron transfer between solid zinc metal and aqueous hydronium ions in dilute sulfuric acid, liberating hydrogen gas bubbles.

MATERIALS:
- Clean zinc metal strip
- Dilute sulfuric acid (H2SO4, 1.0 M)
- Small glass beaker (100 mL)
- Wooden splint & matches

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Define oxidation (loss of electrons: Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻) and reduction (gain of electrons: 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → H2)
- Formulate the net ionic equation: Zn(s) + 2H⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + H2(g)
- Observe direct electron transfer at the metal-solution interface without an external circuit

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Pour 40 mL of dilute sulfuric acid into a clean 100 mL beaker.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: sulfuric_acid_bottle
DROP_TARGET_ID: reaction_beaker
RESULT_COLOR: #f0fdf4
RESULT_STATE: clear_acid_solution
WHY: Dilute sulfuric acid completely dissociates into H⁺ and SO4²⁻ ions in aqueous solution.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Immerse the cleaned zinc strip partially into the dilute sulfuric acid in the beaker.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: zinc_strip
DROP_TARGET_ID: reaction_beaker
RESULT_COLOR: #e2e8f0
RESULT_STATE: active_gas_bubbles_on_zinc
WHY: Direct electron transfer occurs: zinc atoms donate electrons to hydrogen ions, evolving hydrogen gas bubbles directly on the zinc surface.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Test the collected gas bubbles with a lighted wooden splint.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: lighted_splint
DROP_TARGET_ID: reaction_beaker
RESULT_COLOR: #f59e0b
RESULT_STATE: squeaky_pop_flame
WHY: Hydrogen gas burns rapidly with atmospheric oxygen, making a characteristic squeaky 'pop'.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: In the reaction Zn(s) + H2SO4(aq) → ZnSO4(aq) + H2(g), which chemical species undergoes oxidation?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Zinc atoms (Zn loses 2 electrons to become Zn²⁺ ions)|Hydrogen ions (H⁺ loses electrons)|Sulfate ions (SO4²⁻ loses oxygen)|Water molecules
CORRECT_OPTION: Zinc atoms (Zn loses 2 electrons to become Zn²⁺ ions)
RESULT_COLOR: #0284c7
RESULT_STATE: oxidation_half_reaction_verified
HINT: Oxidation is the loss of electrons (OIL RIG: Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain).
WHY: Neutral zinc metal atoms lose two valence electrons each (Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻) to hydronium ions, meaning zinc is oxidized.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Zinc metal reduces aqueous hydrogen ions to hydrogen gas while being oxidized to zinc ions (Zn + H2SO4 → ZnSO4 + H2), demonstrating direct redox electron transfer.
