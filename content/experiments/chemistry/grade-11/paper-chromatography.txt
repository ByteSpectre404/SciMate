TITLE: Paper Chromatography of Plant Pigments
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Separation Techniques
DIFFICULTY: hard
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Separate the constituent pigments (chlorophyll a, chlorophyll b, xanthophyll, carotene) in spinach leaf extract using paper chromatography.

MATERIALS:
- Chromatography paper strip
- Concentrated spinach leaf extract
- Capillary tube
- Solvent (Acetone / Petrol)
- Boiling tube with stopper & hook

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Understand principles of paper chromatography (stationary vs mobile phase)
- Separate mixed plant pigments based on differential solubility and adsorption

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Use a capillary tube to spot crushed spinach extract near the bottom baseline of the paper strip.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: capillary_tube
DROP_TARGET_ID: chromatography_paper_baseline
RESULT_COLOR: #15803d
RESULT_STATE: dark_green_baseline_spot
WHY: Concentrating a small spot at the baseline provides a crisp starting point for component separation.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Suspend the chromatography paper in a boiling tube so the bottom edge dips into the solvent without submerging the spot.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_connect
DRAG_ITEM_ID: chromatography_paper
DROP_TARGET_ID: boiling_tube_hook
RESULT_COLOR: #fef08a
RESULT_STATE: paper_dipped_in_solvent
WHY: Solvent ascends up paper fibers via capillary action, carrying pigments at different speeds.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Allow solvent to travel up the paper strip for 15 minutes.
ACTION_TYPE: toggle_switch
DRAG_ITEM_ID: chromatography_timer
DROP_TARGET_ID: boiling_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #eab308
RESULT_STATE: chromatogram_separated_bands
WHY: As solvent flows upward, pigments partition between stationary cellulose paper and mobile organic solvent.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Observe the separated bands on the developed chromatogram. What do you see?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Distinct colored bands (yellow, blue-green, yellow-green, orange)|Single uniform green smear|White blank paper|Black charcoal stain
CORRECT_OPTION: Distinct colored bands (yellow, blue-green, yellow-green, orange)
RESULT_COLOR: #eab308
RESULT_STATE: chromatogram_separated_bands
HINT: Different pigments migrate to different heights forming colored bands.
WHY: Plant extract contains multiple pigments (carotene, xanthophyll, chlorophyll a & b) with different solubilities, separating into distinct bands.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Paper chromatography separates leaf extract into distinct pigment bands (chlorophylls, xanthophyll, carotene) based on solubility and paper affinity.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- What is the stationary phase and mobile phase in this chromatography set-up?
- Why do different pigments travel different distances up the paper?
