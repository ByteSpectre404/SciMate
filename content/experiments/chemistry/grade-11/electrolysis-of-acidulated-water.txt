TITLE: Electrolysis of Acidulated Water (Decomposition of Water)
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Electrochemistry (Unit 12)
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Decompose acidulated water by electrolysis using inert carbon electrodes, collecting hydrogen and oxygen in inverted tubes to verify the 2:1 volume ratio (2H2O → 2H2 + O2).

MATERIALS:
- Plastic voltameter cup / cell fitted with two sealed carbon electrodes
- Distilled water with a few drops of dilute sulfuric acid (acidulated water)
- Two identical graduated test tubes filled with water and inverted over electrodes
- 9.0 V DC power supply & connecting wires
- Wooden splint & matches

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Assemble an electrolysis apparatus for quantitative gas collection
- Write half-equations: Cathode (4H⁺ + 4e⁻ → 2H2) and Anode (4OH⁻ → 2H2O + O2 + 4e⁻)
- Demonstrate that water consists of hydrogen and oxygen in a 2:1 ratio by volume

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Invert two water-filled graduated test tubes over the cathode and anode in the acidulated water cell.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: inverted_graduated_tubes
DROP_TARGET_ID: voltameter_cell
RESULT_COLOR: #bae6fd
RESULT_STATE: inverted_tubes_primed
WHY: Inverted water-filled tubes collect evolved gases by downward displacement of water for precise volume comparison.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Connect the 9 V DC battery to power the electrolysis and run for 10 minutes.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: dc_power_supply
DROP_TARGET_ID: voltameter_cell
RESULT_COLOR: #3b82f6
RESULT_STATE: steady_gas_collection
WHY: Electric current decomposes water: H⁺ ions migrate to the negative cathode (H2 gas) and OH⁻ ions migrate to the positive anode (O2 gas).

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Measure the volume of gas collected above the cathode and anode.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: metric_scale
DROP_TARGET_ID: voltameter_cell
RESULT_COLOR: #22c55e
RESULT_STATE: volume_ratio_2_to_1
WHY: The cathode collects 20 mL of hydrogen gas while the anode collects 10 mL of oxygen gas (exact 2:1 volume ratio).

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Test the gas collected at the cathode (pop test) and at the anode (glowing splint test).
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: wooden_splint
DROP_TARGET_ID: inverted_graduated_tubes
RESULT_COLOR: #f59e0b
RESULT_STATE: cathode_pops_anode_relights
WHY: Hydrogen at the cathode burns with a squeaky pop; oxygen at the anode relights the glowing splint.

===STEP===
ID: step-5
INSTRUCTION: What is the ratio of the volume of hydrogen gas collected at the cathode to the volume of oxygen gas at the anode?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: 2 : 1 (two volumes of hydrogen to one volume of oxygen)|1 : 1 (equal volumes)|1 : 2 (two volumes of oxygen to one volume of hydrogen)|4 : 1
CORRECT_OPTION: 2 : 1 (two volumes of hydrogen to one volume of oxygen)
RESULT_COLOR: #16a34a
RESULT_STATE: stoichiometry_verified
HINT: The chemical formula for water is H2O: 2H2O(l) → 2H2(g) + O2(g).
WHY: The stoichiometry of water decomposition (2H2O → 2H2 + O2) yields 2 moles of H2 gas for every 1 mole of O2 gas, producing a 2:1 gas volume ratio at equal temperature and pressure.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Electrolysis of acidulated water produces hydrogen gas at the cathode and oxygen gas at the anode in an exact 2:1 volume ratio, confirming the chemical formula H2O.
