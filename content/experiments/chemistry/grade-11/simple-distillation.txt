TITLE: Simple Distillation using a Liebig Condenser
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Separation Techniques
DIFFICULTY: hard
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Separate pure water from a salt solution by boiling and condensing water vapor using a Liebig condenser.

MATERIALS:
- Distillation flask with salt water
- Liebig condenser
- Heat source
- Thermometer
- Receiving flask
- Cold water supply hoses

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Understand distillation principles (vaporization followed by condensation)
- Collect pure distilled solvent separated from non-volatile solutes

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Connect cold water inlet hose to the bottom port of the Liebig condenser jacket.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_connect
DRAG_ITEM_ID: water_inlet_hose
DROP_TARGET_ID: condenser_bottom_port
RESULT_COLOR: #38bdf8
RESULT_STATE: cooling_water_circulating
WHY: Water entering from bottom ensures condenser jacket stays completely filled for efficient cooling.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Heat salt water solution in the distillation flask until it boils.
ACTION_TYPE: toggle_switch
DRAG_ITEM_ID: heat_source
DROP_TARGET_ID: distillation_flask
RESULT_COLOR: #e0f2fe
RESULT_STATE: boiling_steam_generation
EXPECTED_VALUE: 100.0
WHY: Water boils at 100°C producing steam vapor while non-volatile salt remains in flask.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: What happens as hot steam enters the cold Liebig condenser tube?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Steam condenses into liquid water droplets|Steam turns into solid ice|Steam reacts forming gas|No change occurs
CORRECT_OPTION: Steam condenses into liquid water droplets
RESULT_COLOR: #38bdf8
RESULT_STATE: condensing_liquid_droplets
EXPECTED_VALUE: 100.0
HINT: Cooling converts gas vapor into liquid condensate.
WHY: Cold water circulating around inner tube cools steam below 100°C, condensing it into pure liquid water.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Collect distillate liquid in receiving flask. Is the collected water salty or pure?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Pure distilled water (salt-free)|Salty water|Muddy water|Acidic liquid
CORRECT_OPTION: Pure distilled water (salt-free)
RESULT_COLOR: #38bdf8
RESULT_STATE: pure_distilled_water
EXPECTED_VALUE: 100.0
HINT: Salt does not evaporate at water's boiling point.
WHY: Salt remains behind in distillation flask because it has a high boiling point (1413°C), leaving distillate pure.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Distillation boils water into steam and condenses it in a Liebig condenser, yielding pure distilled water free of non-volatile salts.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why must cooling water enter the bottom of the Liebig condenser rather than top?
- How does distillation differ from simple evaporation?
