TITLE: Exothermic and Endothermic Chemical Reactions
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Heat Changes in Reactions
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Compare temperature changes in reactions between magnesium and HCl (exothermic) vs citric acid and sodium bicarbonate (endothermic).

MATERIALS:
- Magnesium ribbon
- Dilute hydrochloric acid (HCl)
- Citric acid solution
- Sodium bicarbonate solution
- Beakers
- Thermometer

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Distinguish exothermic reactions (heat evolved, temp rises) from endothermic reactions (heat absorbed, temp drops)
- Record enthalpy temperature changes systematically

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Measure initial temperature of dilute HCl in beaker, then add magnesium ribbon.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: magnesium_ribbon
DROP_TARGET_ID: hcl_beaker
RESULT_COLOR: #e0f2fe
RESULT_STATE: effervescent
EXPECTED_VALUE: 45.0
WHY: Reacting metal with acid breaks and forms bonds releasing stored chemical energy.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Monitor thermometer during reaction between Mg and HCl. What happens to temperature?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Temperature rises significantly|Temperature drops below zero|Temperature remains constant|Solution freezes
CORRECT_OPTION: Temperature rises significantly
RESULT_COLOR: #e0f2fe
RESULT_STATE: effervescent
EXPECTED_VALUE: 45.0
HINT: Heat is evolved during exothermic metal-acid displacement.
WHY: Mg + 2HCl -> MgCl2 + H2 + Heat. Bond formation releases energy to surroundings, raising temperature (exothermic).

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Mix citric acid solution and sodium bicarbonate solution in a fresh beaker.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: sodium_bicarbonate_solution
DROP_TARGET_ID: citric_acid_beaker
RESULT_COLOR: #f8fafc
RESULT_STATE: effervescent_endothermic
EXPECTED_VALUE: 15.0
WHY: Citric acid reacts with bicarbonate absorbing thermal energy from solution.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Monitor thermometer during citric acid & bicarbonate reaction. What happens to temperature?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Temperature drops significantly|Temperature rises significantly|Beaker bursts into flame|No change
CORRECT_OPTION: Temperature drops significantly
RESULT_COLOR: #f8fafc
RESULT_STATE: effervescent_endothermic
EXPECTED_VALUE: 15.0
HINT: Heat energy is absorbed from surroundings in endothermic reactions.
WHY: Breaking reactant bonds requires more energy than product bond formation, absorbing heat from surroundings and dropping temperature (endothermic).

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Magnesium with HCl releases heat (exothermic, temp rises), whereas citric acid with sodium bicarbonate absorbs heat (endothermic, temp drops).
