TITLE: Identification of Acids and Bases using Indicators
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Acids, Bases and Salts
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Test household and lab solutions (lime juice, HCl, NaOH, soap) using blue/red litmus, methyl orange, and phenolphthalein.

MATERIALS:
- Dilute hydrochloric acid (HCl)
- Dilute sodium hydroxide (NaOH)
- Lime juice
- Soap solution
- Blue litmus paper
- Red litmus paper
- Phenolphthalein indicator

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Classify solutions as acidic or basic using litmus and chemical indicators
- Observe phenolphthalein color response in basic solutions (pink) vs acidic solutions (colorless)

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Dip blue litmus paper into dilute hydrochloric acid (HCl).
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: blue_litmus_paper
DROP_TARGET_ID: hcl_solution
RESULT_COLOR: #ef4444
RESULT_STATE: litmus_red
WHY: Litmus is a plant dye indicator sensitive to hydrogen ion (H+) concentration.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: What color change occurs on blue litmus paper in dilute HCl?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Turns red|Stays blue|Turns bright yellow|Turns purple
CORRECT_OPTION: Turns red
RESULT_COLOR: #ef4444
RESULT_STATE: litmus_red
HINT: Acids turn blue litmus red.
WHY: Acids donate H+ ions which convert blue litmus dye structure to red form.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Add 2 drops of phenolphthalein indicator to dilute sodium hydroxide (NaOH).
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: phenolphthalein_indicator
DROP_TARGET_ID: naoh_solution
RESULT_COLOR: #ec4899
RESULT_STATE: phenolphthalein_pink
WHY: Phenolphthalein is a sensitive indicator for alkaline/basic hydroxide (OH-) solutions.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: What color does phenolphthalein turn in basic sodium hydroxide (NaOH)?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Pink / Magenta|Colorless|Dark Blue|Yellow
CORRECT_OPTION: Pink / Magenta
RESULT_COLOR: #ec4899
RESULT_STATE: phenolphthalein_pink
HINT: Phenolphthalein turns pink in basic solutions (pH > 8.2).
WHY: In alkaline solutions containing excess OH- ions, phenolphthalein molecules become ionized and turn deep pink.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Acids (HCl, lime juice) turn blue litmus red and leave phenolphthalein colorless; bases (NaOH, soap) turn red litmus blue and phenolphthalein pink.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- What color would phenolphthalein turn if added to lime juice?
- What is formed when an acid reacts with a base in neutralisation?
