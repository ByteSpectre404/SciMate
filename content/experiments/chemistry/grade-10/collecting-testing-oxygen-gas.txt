TITLE: Preparation, Collection and Testing of Oxygen Gas
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Changes in Matter (Unit 16)
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Prepare oxygen gas by the thermal decomposition of potassium permanganate (KMnO4), collect over water, and confirm using a glowing wooden splint.

MATERIALS:
- Hard glass boiling tube with delivery tube & retort stand
- Potassium permanganate crystals (KMnO4)
- Water trough & test tubes filled with water
- Bunsen burner
- Glowing wooden splint (ekel)

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Assemble laboratory thermal decomposition apparatus for gas generation
- Write the equation: 2KMnO4(s) → K2MnO4(s) + MnO2(s) + O2(g)
- Perform the glowing splint relighting test for oxygen gas

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Add dark purple KMnO4 crystals into the boiling tube, clamp horizontally, and fit the delivery tube.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: kmno4_crystals
DROP_TARGET_ID: boiling_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #581c87
RESULT_STATE: loaded_kmno4_apparatus
WHY: Clamping with a slight downward incline prevents condensed moisture from cracking the hot tube during heating.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Invert water-filled collection tubes in the water trough over the delivery tube outlet.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: test_tube_water
DROP_TARGET_ID: water_trough
RESULT_COLOR: #bae6fd
RESULT_STATE: ready_for_gas_collection
WHY: Oxygen is sparingly soluble in water and can be collected cleanly over water by downward displacement.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Heat the KMnO4 crystals strongly using the Bunsen burner flame.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: bunsen_burner
DROP_TARGET_ID: boiling_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #1e1b4b
RESULT_STATE: oxygen_gas_bubbling_active
WHY: Thermal decomposition occurs (2KMnO4 → K2MnO4 + MnO2 + O2↑), releasing pure oxygen gas into the inverted tube.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Remove the gas-filled tube from water and quickly insert a glowing wooden splint.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: glowing_splint
DROP_TARGET_ID: ready_for_gas_collection
RESULT_COLOR: #f59e0b
RESULT_STATE: glowing_splint_relights_brightly
WHY: Oxygen strongly supports combustion, rekindling the glowing charcoal ember on the splint into an active bright flame.

===STEP===
ID: step-5
INSTRUCTION: What is the conclusive observation that confirms a gas sample is oxygen?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: It rekindles (relights) a glowing wooden splint with a bright flame|It burns with a squeaky pop|It turns lime water cloudy white|It has a pungent choking smell
CORRECT_OPTION: It rekindles (relights) a glowing wooden splint with a bright flame
RESULT_COLOR: #d97706
RESULT_STATE: oxygen_test_conclusive
HINT: Oxygen gas is not combustible itself, but it is a strong supporter of combustion.
WHY: Relighting a glowing splint is the standard diagnostic laboratory test for oxygen gas.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Oxygen gas liberated from the thermal decomposition of potassium permanganate is collected by downward displacement of water and confirmed by relighting a glowing splint.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why does oxygen relight a glowing splint?
- Why must the delivery tube be removed from the water trough before turning off the Bunsen burner flame (preventing suck-back)?
