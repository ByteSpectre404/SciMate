TITLE: Effect of Temperature on Rate of Reaction
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Rate of Reactions (Unit 17)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Investigate the effect of temperature on the rate of redox reaction between acidified potassium permanganate (KMnO4) solution and clean iron nails.

MATERIALS:
- Very dilute potassium permanganate solution (KMnO4)
- Dilute sulfuric acid (H2SO4)
- Cleaned iron nails of identical size
- Two boiling tubes & Bunsen burner
- Thermometer & stopwatch

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Understand how thermal energy increases the kinetic energy and speed of reacting particles
- Explain activation energy and the fraction of collisions exceeding activation threshold
- Measure the time required for purple KMnO4 to decolorize at room temperature versus elevated temperature

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Add 10 mL dilute KMnO4 and 2 mL dilute H2SO4 to Tube 1 (Cold / Room Temp, 25°C) and Tube 2 (Hot, 60°C).
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: acidified_kmno4_solution
DROP_TARGET_ID: boiling_tubes_pair
RESULT_COLOR: #7e22ce
RESULT_STATE: purple_acidified_solutions
WHY: Acid provides H+ ions necessary for permanganate reduction: MnO4- + 8H+ + 5e- → Mn2+ + 4H2O.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Heat Tube 2 gently over the Bunsen burner until the solution reaches 60°C.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: bunsen_burner
DROP_TARGET_ID: tube_2_hot
RESULT_COLOR: #9333ea
RESULT_STATE: heated_tube_60c
WHY: Heating increases the average kinetic energy and velocity of ions in the solution.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Drop an identical cleaned iron nail into each tube simultaneously and start the stopwatch.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: iron_nails_pair
DROP_TARGET_ID: boiling_tubes_pair
RESULT_COLOR: #f8fafc
RESULT_STATE: decolorizing_reaction_comparison
WHY: In Tube 2 (Hot), the purple solution decolorizes to very pale pink/colorless much faster than in Tube 1 (Cold).

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Why does the reaction occur faster at a higher temperature?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Higher temperature increases kinetic energy, collision frequency, and the fraction of collisions with activation energy|Heat destroys the reactants|Cold slows down the stopwatch|Heat reduces the mass of the iron nail
CORRECT_OPTION: Higher temperature increases kinetic energy, collision frequency, and the fraction of collisions with activation energy
RESULT_COLOR: #a855f7
RESULT_STATE: temperature_rate_effect_proven
HINT: Molecules move faster and collide harder at higher temperatures, so a larger fraction of collisions successfully react.
WHY: Rising temperature increases particle kinetic energy, leading to more frequent collisions and a significantly higher proportion of collisions having energy ≥ activation energy (Ea).

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Increasing temperature increases the rate of chemical reactions by increasing molecular kinetic energy and the proportion of effective collisions per unit time.
