TITLE: Preparation, Collection and Testing of Hydrogen Gas
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Changes in Matter (Unit 16)
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Prepare hydrogen gas in the laboratory by reacting active metals with dilute acid, collect the gas over water by downward displacement of water, and test with a lighted splint.

MATERIALS:
- Boiling tube with one-hole rubber stopper and delivery tube
- Water trough & test tubes filled with water
- Zinc granules / cleaned iron nails
- Dilute hydrochloric acid (HCl)
- Wooden splint (ekel) & matches

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Assemble gas preparation and collection apparatus using downward displacement of water
- Understand why hydrogen can be collected over water (low density, practically insoluble in water)
- Perform the characteristic 'squeaky pop' flame test for hydrogen gas

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Invert a water-filled test tube over the beehive shelf in the water trough.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: test_tube_water
DROP_TARGET_ID: water_trough
RESULT_COLOR: #bae6fd
RESULT_STATE: inverted_water_filled_tube
WHY: Inverted water tubes allow gases insoluble in water to displace liquid downward under atmospheric pressure.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Add zinc granules into the boiling tube containing dilute hydrochloric acid and insert the stopper.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: zinc_granules
DROP_TARGET_ID: boiling_tube_acid
RESULT_COLOR: #f8fafc
RESULT_STATE: active_h2_generation
WHY: The reaction Zn(s) + 2HCl(aq) → ZnCl2(aq) + H2(g) produces hydrogen gas that flows through the delivery tube.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Position the delivery tube end under the inverted test tube to collect hydrogen by downward displacement of water.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: delivery_tube
DROP_TARGET_ID: inverted_water_filled_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #e0f2fe
RESULT_STATE: gas_collected_tube_full
WHY: Hydrogen is less dense than water and insoluble in it, collecting at the top and displacing water downwards.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Remove the gas-filled tube, keep its mouth covered, and introduce a lighted wooden splint.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: lighted_splint
DROP_TARGET_ID: gas_collected_tube_full
RESULT_COLOR: #f59e0b
RESULT_STATE: squeaky_pop_sound_flame
WHY: Hydrogen burns rapidly with atmospheric oxygen (2H2 + O2 → 2H2O), producing a characteristic squeaky 'pop' explosion.

===STEP===
ID: step-5
INSTRUCTION: Which diagnostic test uniquely confirms the presence of hydrogen gas?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Burns with a characteristic squeaky 'pop' sound|Relights a glowing splint|Turns lime water milky|Bleaches moist litmus paper white
CORRECT_OPTION: Burns with a characteristic squeaky 'pop' sound
RESULT_COLOR: #ea580c
RESULT_STATE: hydrogen_identity_confirmed
HINT: Hydrogen is combustible and reacts explosively in small amounts with oxygen when ignited.
WHY: The squeaky 'pop' sound produced upon ignition with a lighted splint is the standard diagnostic test for hydrogen gas.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Hydrogen gas generated from zinc and dilute acid is collected by downward displacement of water and confirmed by its characteristic squeaky 'pop' upon ignition.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why is hydrogen gas collected by downward displacement of water rather than downward displacement of air?
- What chemical compound is formed when hydrogen burns in air?
