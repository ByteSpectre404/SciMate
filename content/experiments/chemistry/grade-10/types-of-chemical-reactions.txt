TITLE: Variety of Chemical Reaction Types
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Changes in Matter (Unit 16)
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Investigate the four main classes of chemical reactions: combination, thermal decomposition, single displacement, and double displacement (precipitation).

MATERIALS:
- Magnesium ribbon & crucible tongs
- Potassium permanganate crystals (KMnO4) & boiling tube with glowing splint
- Copper sulphate solution (CuSO4) & clean zinc granule
- Barium chloride solution (BaCl2) & sodium sulphate solution (Na2SO4)
- Bunsen burner & test tube rack

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Classify reactions into Combination, Decomposition, Single Displacement, and Double Displacement
- Write balanced chemical equations for each reaction type
- Identify precipitates, color transitions, and gas evolution

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Burn magnesium ribbon in air using tongs over the Bunsen flame (Combination Reaction).
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: magnesium_ribbon
DROP_TARGET_ID: bunsen_burner
RESULT_COLOR: #ffffff
RESULT_STATE: combination_2mg_o2_mgo
WHY: Two elements combine to form a single compound: 2Mg(s) + O2(g) → 2MgO(s) (Chemical combination).

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Heat purple KMnO4 crystals in a boiling tube and test the released gas with a glowing splint (Decomposition).
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: kmno4_boiling_tube
DROP_TARGET_ID: bunsen_burner
RESULT_COLOR: #1e293b
RESULT_STATE: decomposition_oxygen_relights_splint
WHY: Heat breaks down one reactant into multiple products: 2KMnO4(s) → K2MnO4(s) + MnO2(s) + O2(g); oxygen relights the glowing splint.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Drop a zinc granule into blue copper sulphate solution in a test tube (Single Displacement).
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: zinc_granule
DROP_TARGET_ID: cuso4_test_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #92400e
RESULT_STATE: displacement_zn_cuso4_brown_cu
WHY: More reactive zinc displaces less reactive copper: Zn(s) + CuSO4(aq) → ZnSO4(aq) + Cu(s) (Blue fades to colourless, brown copper deposits).

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Mix colourless barium chloride solution with colourless sodium sulphate solution (Double Displacement).
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: bacl2_solution
DROP_TARGET_ID: na2so4_test_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #f8fafc
RESULT_STATE: precipitation_white_baso4
WHY: Ions exchange partners to form an insoluble white precipitate: BaCl2(aq) + Na2SO4(aq) → BaSO4(s)↓ + 2NaCl(aq) (Double decomposition).

===STEP===
ID: step-5
INSTRUCTION: What type of chemical reaction occurs when BaCl2 and Na2SO4 form a white precipitate of BaSO4?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Double displacement (Precipitation) reaction|Chemical combination reaction|Thermal decomposition reaction|Single displacement reaction
CORRECT_OPTION: Double displacement (Precipitation) reaction
RESULT_COLOR: #e2e8f0
RESULT_STATE: reaction_classes_verified
HINT: In this reaction, positive and negative ions exchange mutual partners between two aqueous compounds.
WHY: Double displacement occurs when two compounds in aqueous solution exchange ions to produce an insoluble precipitate and a new salt solution.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Chemical reactions are categorized into Combination (A + B → AB), Decomposition (AB → A + B), Single Displacement (A + BC → AC + B), and Double Displacement (AB + CD → AD + CB).
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why does the blue color of copper sulfate fade when zinc is introduced?
- What test confirms that oxygen gas was liberated during the thermal decomposition of KMnO4?
