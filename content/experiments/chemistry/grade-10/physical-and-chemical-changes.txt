TITLE: Distinguishing Physical and Chemical Changes
SUBJECT: chemistry
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Changes in Matter (Unit 16)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Compare physical changes (sublimation and deposition of naphthalene) with chemical changes (burning magnesium, acid-base neutralization, and metal-acid reactions).

MATERIALS:
- Naphthalene moth ball & metal spoons
- Cleaned magnesium ribbon & pair of tongs
- Bunsen burner
- Dilute sulfuric acid & sodium hydroxide pellets
- Thermometer & small beakers

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Distinguish between reversible physical state changes and irreversible chemical reactions
- Recognize indicators of chemical changes (gas evolution, temperature change, permanent color change)
- Formulate word and chemical equations for combustion and neutralization

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Place a moth ball on a heated metal spoon and quickly cover it with another cold spoon.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: naphthalene_ball
DROP_TARGET_ID: heated_metal_spoon
RESULT_COLOR: #f8fafc
RESULT_STATE: sublimated_deposited_naphthalene
WHY: Solid naphthalene sublimes into vapor and deposits as crystalline solid on the cold spoon without forming any new chemical substance (physical change).

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Hold a strip of cleaned magnesium ribbon with crucible tongs in the Bunsen flame.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: magnesium_ribbon
DROP_TARGET_ID: bunsen_burner
RESULT_COLOR: #ffffff
RESULT_STATE: brilliant_white_flash_white_ash
WHY: Magnesium reacts vigorously with atmospheric oxygen in an exothermic combustion: 2Mg(s) + O2(g) → 2MgO(s), forming a new white magnesium oxide powder.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Add sodium hydroxide pellets to dilute sulfuric acid in a beaker and measure the temperature with a thermometer.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: naoh_pellets
DROP_TARGET_ID: acid_beaker
RESULT_COLOR: #fee2e2
RESULT_STATE: exothermic_neutralization_temperature_rise
WHY: Acid-base neutralization 2NaOH + H2SO4 → Na2SO4 + 2H2O releases heat energy, raising the thermometer reading.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Which of the following observations represents a physical change rather than a chemical change?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Naphthalene melting and solidifying again on the cold spoon|Magnesium burning with a bright flame to form white ash|Dilute acid reacting with NaOH to generate heat|Magnesium dissolving in acid to produce hydrogen gas bubbles
CORRECT_OPTION: Naphthalene melting and solidifying again on the cold spoon
RESULT_COLOR: #0284c7
RESULT_STATE: physical_vs_chemical_proven
HINT: In a physical change, only the arrangement or state of particles changes, with no new chemical substances created.
WHY: Sublimation and condensation of naphthalene only alter its physical state (solid ⇄ gas); the chemical composition remains unaltered.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Physical changes alter only physical states or particle arrangements without generating new substances, while chemical changes produce new substances accompanied by energy changes and gas or precipitate formation.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why is the combustion of magnesium ribbon considered a chemical change?
- What are four common signs that a chemical reaction has taken place?
