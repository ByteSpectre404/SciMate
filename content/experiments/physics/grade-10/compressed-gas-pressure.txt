TITLE: Demonstrating Pressure Exerted by Compressed Gas
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Hydrostatic Pressure and Applications (Unit 15)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Demonstrate that confined, compressed air exerts pressure on a liquid surface using an open-arm U-tube liquid manometer and an inflated balloon.

MATERIALS:
- U-tube manometer
- Colored water
- Inflated air balloon with tied thread
- Stand and clamp
- Metric scale

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Explain how a U-tube manometer measures gauge pressure differences
- Understand that compressed gases exert outward force per unit surface area
- Relate liquid column displacement Δh to excess gas pressure ΔP = hρg

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Pour colored water into the U-tube and verify that initial liquid levels in arms X and Y are level.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: colored_water_beaker
DROP_TARGET_ID: u_tube_manometer
RESULT_COLOR: #38bdf8
RESULT_STATE: balanced_u_tube_manometer
WHY: In an open U-tube, equal atmospheric pressure acts on both arms, causing equal resting heights.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Attach the neck of the inflated air balloon tightly over arm X of the U-tube.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: inflated_balloon
DROP_TARGET_ID: u_tube_manometer
RESULT_COLOR: #f97316
RESULT_STATE: connected_sealed_balloon
WHY: Securing the balloon creates an airtight junction between the compressed gas reservoir and arm X.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Untie the restraining thread to release compressed air from the balloon into arm X.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: lab_scissors
DROP_TARGET_ID: inflated_balloon
RESULT_COLOR: #0284c7
RESULT_STATE: displaced_liquid_manometer
WHY: High pressure in the balloon exerts a downward thrust on the liquid in arm X, driving liquid upward in open arm Y.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: What happens to the liquid levels in arm X and arm Y upon releasing the knot?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Level in arm X goes down while level in arm Y rises|Levels in both arms rise equally|Levels in both arms remain unchanged|Level in arm X rises while arm Y drops
CORRECT_OPTION: Level in arm X goes down while level in arm Y rises
RESULT_COLOR: #0ea5e9
RESULT_STATE: pressure_differential_verified
HINT: The compressed gas pushes down on the liquid surface in arm X, forcing liquid up into arm Y.
WHY: The compressed air exerts a pressure exceeding atmospheric pressure, displacing water by a height difference Δh corresponding to ΔP = hρg.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Compressed air exerts pressure proportional to its confinement. The excess pressure displaces the liquid in the U-tube manometer until hydrostatic pressure balances gas pressure.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- How does increasing the volume of air compressed in the balloon affect the height difference between arms X and Y?
- If mercury were used instead of water in the manometer, how would the column displacement change?
