TITLE: Gravitational Potential Energy and Mass
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Work, Energy and Power (Unit 18)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Demonstrate that gravitational potential energy stored in an object increases in direct proportion to its mass when released from a constant vertical height.

MATERIALS:
- Smooth modeling clay bed (3 cm thick)
- Plastic bottle (empty / 1/4 sand / 1/2 sand / fully filled with sand)
- Sand reservoir
- Meter ruler

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Understand the relationship Ep = mgh where mass (m) is varied
- Observe how changing mass influences the kinetic energy at impact
- Deduce that Ep is directly proportional to mass for a fixed height

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Fill 1/4 of the plastic bottle with sand and release it onto the flat clay from a height of 1.0 m.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: bottle_quarter_sand
DROP_TARGET_ID: clay_bed
RESULT_COLOR: #eab308
RESULT_STATE: shallow_indent_low_mass
WHY: A small mass possesses relatively low potential energy Ep = mgh, creating a small shallow depression.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Fill the plastic bottle completely with sand and release it from the same 1.0 m height onto fresh clay.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: bottle_full_sand
DROP_TARGET_ID: clay_bed
RESULT_COLOR: #ca8a04
RESULT_STATE: deep_indent_high_mass
WHY: A greater mass possesses significantly more potential energy at the same height, delivering greater impact energy into the clay.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: How does the depth of the depression on the clay change as the mass of the bottle increases?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Depth of depression increases as mass increases|Depth of depression decreases as mass increases|Depth remains identical for all masses|No depression forms because height was held constant
CORRECT_OPTION: Depth of depression increases as mass increases
RESULT_COLOR: #854d0e
RESULT_STATE: ep_proportional_to_mass_verified
HINT: Ep = mgh. With constant g and constant h, potential energy is directly proportional to mass m.
WHY: Gravitational potential energy increases linearly with mass (Ep ∝ m). A heavier falling object does more work upon impact.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: For a fixed vertical height, the depth of depression in clay increases with the mass of the falling object, confirming that Ep = mgh.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why was it necessary to keep the release height constant in this experiment?
- What would happen to the depression depth if both mass and height were doubled simultaneously?
