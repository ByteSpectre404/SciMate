TITLE: Gravitational Potential Energy and Height
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Work, Energy and Power (Unit 18)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Demonstrate that gravitational potential energy stored in an object increases in direct proportion to its release height above the ground using clay depression measurements.

MATERIALS:
- Smooth moist modeling clay bed (3 cm thick)
- Heavy spherical metal weight (e.g. 500 g)
- Meter ruler
- Vernier depth gauge / measuring pin

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Understand gravitational potential energy Ep = mgh
- Relate work done in deforming clay to the kinetic and potential energy of falling masses
- Prove that Ep is directly proportional to vertical height (h)

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Smooth the clay bed into a uniform flat 3 cm thick layer on the floor.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: clay_smoother
DROP_TARGET_ID: clay_bed
RESULT_COLOR: #d97706
RESULT_STATE: flat_clay_layer
WHY: A uniform flat surface provides a consistent baseline for measuring crater depression depth.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Position the metal weight at a height of 0.5 m above the clay and release it vertically from rest.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: metal_weight
DROP_TARGET_ID: clay_bed
RESULT_COLOR: #ca8a04
RESULT_STATE: depression_depth_shallow
WHY: As the weight falls, potential energy converts into kinetic energy, doing work on the clay to form a shallow depression.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Smooth the clay and release the same metal weight from a higher height of 1.5 m.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: metal_weight
DROP_TARGET_ID: clay_bed
RESULT_COLOR: #a16207
RESULT_STATE: depression_depth_deep
WHY: Greater height provides more initial potential energy (Ep = mgh), resulting in greater impact kinetic energy and a deeper depression.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: What happens to the depth of the depression in the clay as the initial release height increases?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Depth of depression increases with greater release height|Depth of depression decreases with greater release height|Depth of depression remains unchanged|The clay bounces the weight without making an indent
CORRECT_OPTION: Depth of depression increases with greater release height
RESULT_COLOR: #78350f
RESULT_STATE: ep_proportional_to_height_verified
HINT: Potential energy Ep = mgh. Higher release points impart more energy to deform the clay upon impact.
WHY: Gravitational potential energy is directly proportional to height (Ep ∝ h). The greater the height, the more energy is transferred to deform the clay.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: The depth of depression in the clay increases with vertical release height, proving that gravitational potential energy increases directly with height (Ep = mgh).
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- What energy transformations occur from the moment of release until the weight comes to rest in the clay?
- If the drop height is doubled, how does the potential energy change?
