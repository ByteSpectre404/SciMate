TITLE: Generating Electric Current from Electrostatic Charges
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 10
UNIT: Current Electricity (Unit 19)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Demonstrate that electric current is the flow of electric charges through a conductor by discharging frictionally charged electrostatic bodies through a sensitive neon indicator bulb.

MATERIALS:
- Rigid PVC rod / tube
- Polythene cloth / rubbing strip
- Miniature neon indicator bulb
- Grounding lead / earth wire
- Insulating stand

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Understand that electric current is defined as the rate of flow of electric charges (I = Q / t)
- Generate static negative charges on PVC via friction (triboelectric charging)
- Observe the transient ionization and glow of a neon gas bulb during charge transfer

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Connect one terminal of the neon bulb to the ground (earth wire) and mount it on the insulating stand.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: earth_wire
DROP_TARGET_ID: neon_bulb
RESULT_COLOR: #94a3b8
RESULT_STATE: grounded_neon_bulb
WHY: Grounding provides a discharge pathway for excess electrons to flow into the earth.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Rub the PVC tube vigorously with the dry polythene cloth to charge it electrostatically.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: polythene_cloth
DROP_TARGET_ID: pvc_tube
RESULT_COLOR: #cbd5e1
RESULT_STATE: charged_pvc_rod
WHY: Friction transfers electrons from the polythene to the PVC rod, leaving the PVC negatively charged.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Touch the charged PVC rod onto the free terminal of the neon bulb.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: pvc_tube
DROP_TARGET_ID: neon_bulb
RESULT_COLOR: #f97316
RESULT_STATE: neon_bulb_flash_glow
WHY: The accumulated electrons on the PVC rod surge through the neon gas into the ground wire, ionizing the gas and producing an orange flash.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Why does the neon bulb briefly flash when touched by the charged PVC rod?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Static charges stored on the PVC flow through the bulb as an electric current|The PVC rod heats up the bulb through friction|The bulb reflects light from the room|Chemical reactions occur between PVC and glass
CORRECT_OPTION: Static charges stored on the PVC flow through the bulb as an electric current
RESULT_COLOR: #ea580c
RESULT_STATE: electric_current_nature_proven
HINT: Current is defined as moving electric charges. When static electrons discharge, they produce a momentary current.
WHY: Electric current is the flow of electric charge carriers (electrons). As charges equalize through the circuit, the brief current illuminates the neon gas.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Static electric charges accumulated on an insulator flow through conductors when a closed path to ground is provided, generating a brief electric current.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why does the neon bulb produce only a brief flash rather than glowing continuously?
- How does continuous current generated by a chemical battery differ from this electrostatic discharge?
