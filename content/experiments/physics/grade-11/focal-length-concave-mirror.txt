TITLE: Focal Length and Image Formation of Concave Mirrors
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Geometrical Optics
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Determine the focal length of a concave mirror by focusing distant light rays and investigate real image formation at varying object distances.

MATERIALS:
- Concave mirror on stand
- White screen on stand
- Lighted candle
- Meter ruler

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Measure focal length (f) using distant object light convergence
- Differentiate real inverted images from virtual upright images formed by curved mirrors

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Point concave mirror towards a distant window and adjust screen until a sharp image of outside scene forms.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: white_screen
DROP_TARGET_ID: concave_mirror_axis
RESULT_COLOR: #f8fafc
RESULT_STATE: inverted_window_image
EXPECTED_VALUE: 15.0
WHY: Light rays from a distant object arrive parallel and converge at the principal focus (F).

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Measure the distance between concave mirror and screen when image is sharp.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_measure
DRAG_ITEM_ID: meter_ruler
DROP_TARGET_ID: mirror_screen_gap
RESULT_COLOR: #f8fafc
RESULT_STATE: focused_focal_length_point
EXPECTED_VALUE: 15.0
WHY: The distance from mirror pole to focus screen equals focal length f.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Place a lighted candle at a distance greater than 2f in front of the mirror.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: lighted_candle
DROP_TARGET_ID: axis_beyond_2f
RESULT_COLOR: #f59e0b
RESULT_STATE: candle_beyond_2f
EXPECTED_VALUE: 15.0
WHY: Placing object beyond 2f tests image formation characteristics.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Move the screen until a clear image of the candle flame appears. What is the nature of the image?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Real, inverted, and diminished|Virtual, upright, and magnified|Real, upright, and magnified|No image forms
CORRECT_OPTION: Real, inverted, and diminished
RESULT_COLOR: #f59e0b
RESULT_STATE: real_inverted_diminished_image
EXPECTED_VALUE: 15.0
HINT: Concave mirrors project inverted real images on screens when u > 2f.
WHY: When object distance u > 2f, concave mirrors form a real, inverted, smaller image between f and 2f on a screen.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Concave mirrors converge parallel rays to a focal point at distance f. Real inverted images form on screens when objects sit beyond f.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why cannot virtual images be projected on a screen?
- What type of image is formed when a candle is placed closer than focal length f to a concave mirror?
