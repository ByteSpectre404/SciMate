TITLE: Magnetic Field Around a Current-Carrying Conductor
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Electromagnetism & Electromagnetic Induction (Unit 13)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Demonstrate Oersted's discovery: an electric current flowing through a straight wire creates a concentric circular magnetic field that deflects a nearby compass needle.

MATERIALS:
- Straight copper conducting wire (aligned North-South)
- Magnetic plotting compass
- DC power supply / two 1.5 V dry cells (3.0 V)
- Knife switch and reversing switch

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Observe Oersted's effect: electric currents generate magnetic fields
- Apply the Right-Hand Grip Rule to determine concentric circular field line direction
- Deduce the effects of current reversal and placing the compass above versus below the wire

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Align the straight copper wire directly over the compass along the magnetic North-South axis with switch open.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: straight_copper_wire
DROP_TARGET_ID: compass_needle
RESULT_COLOR: #94a3b8
RESULT_STATE: parallel_wire_no_current
WHY: With zero current (I = 0), only the Earth's natural magnetic field acts on the compass needle.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Close the switch to send electric current northward through the wire directly above the compass.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: knife_switch
DROP_TARGET_ID: dc_power_circuit
RESULT_COLOR: #3b82f6
RESULT_STATE: compass_deflects_west
WHY: Current creates concentric circular magnetic field lines around the wire; under the wire, the field points West, deflecting the North tip of the needle to the West.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Reverse the direction of current flow (send current southward) and observe the compass.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: polarity_reversing_switch
DROP_TARGET_ID: dc_power_circuit
RESULT_COLOR: #22c55e
RESULT_STATE: compass_deflects_east
WHY: Reversing current direction reverses the circular magnetic flux direction (Right-Hand Grip Rule), deflecting the needle in the opposite direction (East).

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: What happens to the compass needle when the current flowing through the wire is switched OFF?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: The needle immediately swings back to align along Earth's North-South meridian|The needle remains permanently frozen in the deflected position|The needle spins continuously like a motor|The compass loses all magnetism
CORRECT_OPTION: The needle immediately swings back to align along Earth's North-South meridian
RESULT_COLOR: #16a34a
RESULT_STATE: oersted_principle_verified
HINT: The magnetic field exists only while electric charges are actively in motion through the conductor.
WHY: The magnetic field around a conductor is generated exclusively by the motion of electric charges. When current ceases, the field collapses instantly.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: An electric current flowing through a conductor creates a magnetic field around it whose orientation depends on current direction (Right-Hand Grip Rule), proving the fundamental link between electricity and magnetism.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- How does the strength of the magnetic field change as distance from the wire increases?
- How does winding the wire into a multi-turn solenoid intensify the central magnetic field?
