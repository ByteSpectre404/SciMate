TITLE: Thermal Expansion of Solids (Ball and Ring)
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Heat (Unit 9)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Demonstrate the thermal expansion and contraction of solids using Gravesande's metal ring and ball apparatus.

MATERIALS:
- Gravesande's ball and ring apparatus
- Bunsen burner
- Cold water bath / beaker
- Insulated handle & tongs

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Demonstrate that solid metals expand upon heating and contract upon cooling
- Explain thermal expansion in terms of increased particle vibration amplitudes around fixed lattice positions
- Discuss everyday applications and hazards of solid expansion (railway expansion gaps, bimetallic strips, riveting)

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Test the cold iron ball by passing it through the brass ring at room temperature.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: cold_iron_ball
DROP_TARGET_ID: brass_ring
RESULT_COLOR: #cbd5e1
RESULT_STATE: passes_freely_through_ring
WHY: At room temperature, the diameter of the ball is slightly smaller than the internal diameter of the ring.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Heat the metal ball strongly in the Bunsen burner flame for 3 minutes.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: bunsen_burner
DROP_TARGET_ID: cold_iron_ball
RESULT_COLOR: #f97316
RESULT_STATE: hot_expanded_metal_ball
WHY: Heat energy increases the vibrational kinetic energy of metal lattice atoms, increasing interatomic separation and expanding the ball's volume.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Attempt to pass the hot metal ball through the brass ring.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: hot_expanded_metal_ball
DROP_TARGET_ID: brass_ring
RESULT_COLOR: #ef4444
RESULT_STATE: ball_blocked_too_large_to_pass
WHY: Thermal expansion has enlarged the ball's diameter beyond the ring's aperture, preventing it from passing through.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Cool the ball by immersing it in a beaker of cold water and re-test through the ring.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: hot_expanded_metal_ball
DROP_TARGET_ID: cold_water_bath
RESULT_COLOR: #64748b
RESULT_STATE: cooled_ball_passes_freely_again
WHY: Cooling removes kinetic energy; atoms return to their original resting spacing, contracting the ball so it slips through easily.

===STEP===
ID: step-5
INSTRUCTION: What general principle does the Gravesande's ball and ring experiment prove?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Solids expand when heated and contract when cooled|Solids change into liquids whenever their diameter increases|The ring shrinks while the ball remains constant|Metals become heavier when heated in air
CORRECT_OPTION: Solids expand when heated and contract when cooled
RESULT_COLOR: #334155
RESULT_STATE: thermal_expansion_rule_proven
HINT: Heating increases particle vibrations, causing the dimensions of the solid object to expand in all directions.
WHY: Thermal expansion occurs because atoms vibrate with greater amplitudes about mean positions as temperature rises, increasing the bulk volume of solids.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Solid metals expand upon heating (preventing the ball from passing through the ring) and contract upon cooling (allowing it to pass through freely again).
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why are small expansion gaps left between steel railway tracks?
- How does a bimetallic strip made of brass and iron bend when heated in a fire alarm or thermostat?
