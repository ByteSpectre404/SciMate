TITLE: Full-Wave Rectification Using a Diode Bridge
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Electronics (Unit 11)
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Demonstrate full-wave rectification of alternating current (AC) into pulsating direct current (DC) using a four-diode Graetz bridge and a centre-zero galvanometer.

MATERIALS:
- AC source (hand-crank dynamo / bicycle generator / low-voltage AC transformer)
- Four 1N4001 diodes soldered in diamond bridge configuration
- Centre-zero galvanometer (-30 to +30 μA)
- 100 Ω protective rheostat & connecting wires

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Assemble a 4-diode bridge rectifier circuit
- Observe how AC current alternating in two directions is converted into unidirectional pulsating DC
- Trace conduction paths through diode pairs (D1-D2 on positive half-cycle, D3-D4 on negative half-cycle)

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Connect the AC generator directly to the centre-zero galvanometer and rotate slowly.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: ac_generator
DROP_TARGET_ID: galvanometer
RESULT_COLOR: #94a3b8
RESULT_STATE: needle_oscillates_both_directions
WHY: Direct AC current periodically reverses direction, causing the galvanometer needle to swing back and forth on both sides of zero.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Connect the four-diode bridge rectifier between the AC generator and the galvanometer circuit.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: diode_bridge_assembly
DROP_TARGET_ID: circuit_board
RESULT_COLOR: #3b82f6
RESULT_STATE: bridge_circuit_connected
WHY: The diamond bridge directs both halves of the AC waveform through the load in the same single direction.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Rotate the AC generator again and observe the galvanometer deflection.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: ac_generator
DROP_TARGET_ID: diode_bridge_assembly
RESULT_COLOR: #22c55e
RESULT_STATE: unidirectional_pulsing_deflection
WHY: The galvanometer needle now deflects exclusively to one side of zero, confirming that the current flowing through the meter is unidirectional DC.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: What is the principal function of a full-wave diode bridge rectifier?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Converts alternating current (AC) into unidirectional direct current (DC)|Amplifies the AC voltage by 400%|Converts electrical energy into magnetic waves|Reverses direct current into alternating current
CORRECT_OPTION: Converts alternating current (AC) into unidirectional direct current (DC)
RESULT_COLOR: #16a34a
RESULT_STATE: rectification_function_confirmed
HINT: In a bridge rectifier, complementary diode pairs steer both positive and negative AC half-cycles in the same direction through the output load.
WHY: Bridge rectification channels both half-cycles of alternating current through the load in a single direction, converting bidirectional AC to pulsating DC.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: A four-diode bridge rectifier converts alternating current into pulsating direct current by steering both half-cycles through the load in the same direction.
