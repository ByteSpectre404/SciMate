TITLE: Thermal Expansion of Gases
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Heat (Unit 9)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Demonstrate the thermal expansion and contraction of air using an empty bottle with a deflated balloon placed in warm water and ice water baths.

MATERIALS:
- Empty plastic / glass bottle
- Small deflated rubber balloon
- Vessel with hot water
- Vessel with ice-cold water

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Demonstrate that gases expand considerably when heated and contract upon cooling
- Compare the relative expansion rates of gases, liquids, and solids (Gases > Liquids > Solids)
- Explain gas volume changes according to Charles's Law (V ∝ T at constant pressure)

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Fit the deflated balloon securely over the mouth of the empty (air-filled) bottle at room temperature.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: rubber_balloon
DROP_TARGET_ID: empty_bottle
RESULT_COLOR: #cbd5e1
RESULT_STATE: sealed_air_bottle
WHY: The bottle traps a fixed mass of air at constant atmospheric pressure.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Place the bottle into a vessel containing hot water.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: empty_bottle
DROP_TARGET_ID: hot_water_vessel
RESULT_COLOR: #f97316
RESULT_STATE: balloon_inflates_erect
WHY: Thermal energy increases the kinetic energy and speed of air molecules inside, increasing pressure and expanding the volume into the flexible balloon.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Transfer the bottle from hot water into a vessel of ice-cold water.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: empty_bottle
DROP_TARGET_ID: ice_water_vessel
RESULT_COLOR: #38bdf8
RESULT_STATE: balloon_deflates_contracts
WHY: As the air cools, molecules lose kinetic energy and move closer together, reducing the volume and deflating the balloon.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: Why does the balloon inflate when the bottle is placed in hot water?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: The air inside the bottle expands upon heating, filling more volume|Hot water enters the bottle through invisible pores|The rubber stretches due to sunlight|A chemical reaction creates new gas inside the bottle
CORRECT_OPTION: The air inside the bottle expands upon heating, filling more volume
RESULT_COLOR: #ea580c
RESULT_STATE: gas_expansion_law_verified
HINT: Gases expand upon heating because increased kinetic energy drives molecules further apart (Charles's Law: V ∝ T).
WHY: Heating increases molecular motion and collisions, causing the confined air to expand in volume and inflate the balloon.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Gases expand substantially when heated and contract when cooled, expanding more than both liquids and solids for equivalent temperature increases.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- How does the expansion of gases explain why hot air balloons rise in the atmosphere?
- Why should car tire pressure be checked when tires are cold rather than after long driving on hot asphalt?
