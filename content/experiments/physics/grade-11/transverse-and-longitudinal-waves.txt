TITLE: Transverse and Longitudinal Wave Motion
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Waves and Applications
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Demonstrate transverse and longitudinal mechanical wave propagation using a slinky spring and ribbon markers.

MATERIALS:
- Slinky spring
- Colored ribbon markers
- Table surface

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Differentiate transverse wave motion (perpendicular vibration) from longitudinal wave motion (parallel vibration)
- Identify compressions and rarefactions in longitudinal sound wave models

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Tie ribbons along slinky and shake one end left-and-right across the table.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: ribbon_marker
DROP_TARGET_ID: slinky_coils
RESULT_COLOR: #ef4444
RESULT_STATE: transverse_wave_sine
WHY: Ribbon markers make individual particle oscillations clearly visible as waves travel down the slinky.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Observe the ribbon motion during left-and-right slinky shaking.
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Ribbon vibrates perpendicular (90 deg) to wave direction|Ribbon vibrates parallel to wave direction|Ribbon stays completely still|Ribbon rotates in circles
CORRECT_OPTION: Ribbon vibrates perpendicular (90 deg) to wave direction
RESULT_COLOR: #ef4444
RESULT_STATE: transverse_wave_sine
HINT: In transverse waves, particles vibrate perpendicular to wave motion.
WHY: Left-and-right shaking creates transverse waves where particle vibration is perpendicular to wave propagation.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Push and pull slinky end forward and backward along its length.
ACTION_TYPE: toggle_switch
DRAG_ITEM_ID: slinky_push_pull
DROP_TARGET_ID: slinky
RESULT_COLOR: #3b82f6
RESULT_STATE: longitudinal_compression_rarefaction
WHY: Forward-and-backward motion generates compressions (coils bunched) and rarefactions (coils spread).

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: How do ribbon particles move during forward-and-backward pushing?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Ribbon oscillates parallel to wave direction|Ribbon oscillates perpendicular to wave direction|Ribbon moves only upwards|Ribbon does not move
CORRECT_OPTION: Ribbon oscillates parallel to wave direction
RESULT_COLOR: #3b82f6
RESULT_STATE: longitudinal_compression_rarefaction
HINT: Longitudinal waves feature parallel particle oscillation (like sound waves).
WHY: Push-pull motion forms longitudinal waves where medium particles oscillate parallel to wave propagation direction.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Transverse waves move particles perpendicular to wave travel while longitudinal waves move particles parallel to wave travel via compressions and rarefactions.
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- What type of wave is a sound wave traveling through air?
- Which wave property determines the pitch of a sound?
