TITLE: Heat Transfer and Thermal Equilibrium
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Heat (Unit 9)
DIFFICULTY: easy
DURATION_MINUTES: 15
DESCRIPTION: Investigate the direction of heat flow and achievement of thermal equilibrium when a heated iron block is immersed in room temperature water.

MATERIALS:
- Heated iron block (80°C) with tongs
- Insulated calorimeter / vessel with room temperature water (25°C)
- Laboratory thermometer (-10°C to 110°C)
- Stirrer

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Define heat as thermal energy transferring from a hotter body to a colder body
- Understand thermal equilibrium as the state where temperature equalizes and net heat transfer ceases
- Apply the principle of conservation of heat: Heat Lost = Heat Gained

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Record the initial temperature of cold water in the vessel (25°C) with the thermometer.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: lab_thermometer

RESULT_COLOR: #38bdf8
RESULT_STATE: initial_temp_25c
WHY: Establishing the initial temperature provides the baseline for measuring heat absorbed by the water.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Transfer the hot iron block (80°C) into the vessel of cold water using crucible tongs.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: hot_iron_block
DROP_TARGET_ID: water_vessel
RESULT_COLOR: #f97316
RESULT_STATE: heat_transfer_in_progress
WHY: Thermal energy spontaneously flows down the temperature gradient from the high-temperature iron block to the cooler water.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Stir the water gently and monitor the thermometer until the reading stabilizes at 38°C.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: glass_stirrer
DROP_TARGET_ID: water_vessel
RESULT_COLOR: #ea580c
RESULT_STATE: thermal_equilibrium_reached_38c
WHY: Continuous stirring ensures uniform temperature distribution until both iron and water reach a common equilibrium temperature.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: What happens when two objects at different temperatures are placed in thermal contact?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Heat flows from the hotter body to the colder body until both reach the same common temperature (thermal equilibrium)|Heat flows from cold to hot until one freezes|Both objects continuously heat up indefinitely|The temperature difference between them remains permanent
CORRECT_OPTION: Heat flows from the hotter body to the colder body until both reach the same common temperature (thermal equilibrium)
RESULT_COLOR: #c2410c
RESULT_STATE: thermal_equilibrium_law_proven
HINT: Energy flows spontaneously from higher temperature to lower temperature until no temperature gradient remains.
WHY: Heat flows down a thermal gradient until thermal equilibrium is attained, at which point net heat exchange becomes zero.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Heat flows spontaneously from a body at higher temperature to one at lower temperature until both reach a common thermal equilibrium temperature.
