TITLE: Transistor as an Audio Frequency Signal Amplifier
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Electronics (Unit 11)
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Construct a common-emitter single-stage audio amplifier using an NPN transistor, base bias resistor, coupling capacitor, and miniature 8 Ω speaker.

MATERIALS:
- 2SD400 NPN transistor
- 22 kΩ base bias resistor & 0.1 μF coupling capacitor
- 8 Ω miniature loudspeaker
- Audio frequency (AF) tone signal generator
- 3.0 V DC power supply & breadboard

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Assemble a single-stage common-emitter AC voltage and power amplifier
- Understand the function of a coupling capacitor in blocking DC while passing AC audio signals
- Compare direct weak input signal volume with the amplified output sound from the speaker

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Connect the AF signal generator directly to the 8 Ω speaker and listen to the tone volume.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: af_signal_generator
DROP_TARGET_ID: mini_speaker
RESULT_COLOR: #94a3b8
RESULT_STATE: faint_barely_audible_tone
WHY: The weak unamplified audio source cannot deliver sufficient electrical power directly to drive the speaker cone effectively.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Assemble the amplifier circuit: transistor, 22 kΩ base bias resistor, 0.1 μF capacitor, and speaker connected to the collector.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: amplifier_components_kit
DROP_TARGET_ID: breadboard_circuit
RESULT_COLOR: #3b82f6
RESULT_STATE: biased_amplifier_circuit
WHY: The 22 kΩ resistor sets the forward bias operating point on the base, while the capacitor isolates the DC bias from the AC signal source.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Feed the faint AF signal across the input terminals (points A and B) and listen to the speaker output.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: af_signal_generator
DROP_TARGET_ID: amplifier_input_terminals
RESULT_COLOR: #22c55e
RESULT_STATE: loud_amplified_sound_output
WHY: The fluctuating AC voltage at the base causes large proportional fluctuations in collector current, delivering powerful amplified audio power to the speaker.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: What is the primary role of the 0.1 μF capacitor placed in series with the transistor base input?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: It blocks DC bias voltage while allowing AC audio signals to pass through freely|It converts sound waves into light|It recharges the battery constantly|It reduces the loudness of the amplifier
CORRECT_OPTION: It blocks DC bias voltage while allowing AC audio signals to pass through freely
RESULT_COLOR: #16a34a
RESULT_STATE: coupling_capacitor_function_verified
HINT: Capacitors present infinite impedance to steady DC voltage but low impedance to alternating AC frequencies.
WHY: The input coupling capacitor prevents the DC base bias voltage from being shorted to ground or disturbed by the signal source, while coupling the AC audio signal.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: An NPN transistor amplifies weak audio frequency alternating signals into a much stronger AC output power capable of driving acoustic transducers like loudspeakers.
