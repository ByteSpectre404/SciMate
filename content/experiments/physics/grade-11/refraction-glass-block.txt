TITLE: Refraction of Light through a Glass Block
SUBJECT: physics
GRADE: 11
UNIT: Geometrical Optics
DIFFICULTY: medium
DURATION_MINUTES: 20
DESCRIPTION: Trace light rays passing from air into a glass block and back into air to observe refraction toward and away from the normal.

MATERIALS:
- Rectangular glass block
- Drawing board & paper
- Optical pins (A, B, C, D)
- Ruler & protractor
- Pencil

LEARNING_OUTCOMES:
- Trace path of a refracted ray at optical boundaries
- Verify refraction rules (rare to dense bends toward normal; dense to rare bends away)

===STEP===
ID: step-1
INSTRUCTION: Place glass block on paper, trace its outline, and insert pins A and B along an incident ray path.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: optical_pins_ab
DROP_TARGET_ID: incident_line
RESULT_COLOR: #3b82f6
RESULT_STATE: incident_ray_marked
EXPECTED_VALUE: 30.0
WHY: Pins A and B define the direction of the incident light ray approaching the glass boundary.

===STEP===
ID: step-2
INSTRUCTION: Look through opposite glass side and align pins C and D so all 4 pins appear collinear.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_place
DRAG_ITEM_ID: optical_pins_cd
DROP_TARGET_ID: emergent_side
RESULT_COLOR: #3b82f6
RESULT_STATE: emergent_ray_aligned
EXPECTED_VALUE: 30.0
WHY: Sighting through glass places C and D along the line of the emergent refracted ray.

===STEP===
ID: step-3
INSTRUCTION: Draw normals at entry point B and exit point C and join path B-C inside the glass.
ACTION_TYPE: drag_connect
DRAG_ITEM_ID: pencil_ruler
DROP_TARGET_ID: ray_path_bc
RESULT_COLOR: #3b82f6
RESULT_STATE: refracted_ray_drawn
EXPECTED_VALUE: 19.5
WHY: Connecting entry and exit points reveals the actual bent ray path inside the glass medium.

===STEP===
ID: step-4
INSTRUCTION: How does the light ray bend when entering glass from air at entry point B?
ACTION_TYPE: select_observation
OPTIONS: Bends towards the normal|Bends away from the normal|Passes straight without bending|Reflects backwards completely
CORRECT_OPTION: Bends towards the normal
RESULT_COLOR: #3b82f6
RESULT_STATE: refracted_ray_drawn
EXPECTED_VALUE: 19.5
HINT: Glass is optically denser than air, slowing down light.
WHY: Light slows down in denser glass medium causing the ray to refract towards the normal line.

===OUTCOME===
SUMMARY: Light bends towards the normal when entering glass (denser medium) and away from the normal when exiting into air (rarer medium).
REFLECTION_QUESTIONS:
- Why does light bend when moving between air and glass?
- Why is the emergent ray parallel to the incident ray for a rectangular glass block?
